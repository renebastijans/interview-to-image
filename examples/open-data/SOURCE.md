# Source of the open-data example

The example image in `examples/images/example-open-data-panel.jpg` was made from one line in a public interview transcript.

Enriquez, Diana. "Delivery Gig Worker Interviews on Automation at Work." September 2019. Princeton Data Commons. https://doi.org/10.34770/4324-yn77

- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Anonymisation and consent:** the dataset's authors anonymised the transcripts and obtained consent for public sharing.
- **Used here:** one verbatim line from interview UE2 (a delivery driver). No transcript text beyond that line is reproduced in this repository.
- **Changes made:** the line was turned into a comic panel with a fictional composite driver. The driver is not a real interviewee. The image is an adaptation and is not endorsed by the dataset's authors.
- **Caveat:** the interviews are from 2019 and from the US, so pay and conditions may have changed. One quote is not a pattern.
