# Prompt, page 1: The paper list

PROMPT START

Create a new four-panel research comic using the following aesthetic and presentation criteria:

- hand-drawn ink illustration with a soft, muted colour wash: watercolour or coloured pencil laid over a visible pen line, never flat digital fill
- a restrained, desaturated palette: warm greys, ochres, muted browns, soft natural skin tones, with plenty of bare paper left showing through
- screens are the only cool light in the comic: a laptop glows a pale blue-grey against the warm surroundings
- realistic rather than cartoonish characters; fine cross-hatching and sketch shading still visible through the colour
- expressive faces and body language; clean bordered comic panels; hand-lettered comic text; minimal dialogue
- slightly imperfect, wrinkled white paper resting on a wooden surface
- the final panel should carry the greatest emotional weight
- the image should be widescreen 16:9 for a slide

The comic should illustrate **a team's shared planning board is quietly backed up by one person's private paper list.**

**Underlying research finding:** After a team rebuilt its shared board, a client deadline did not carry over and nobody noticed until a colleague asked. Afterwards the operations lead kept a paper list "just in case" and did not tell the team. The board is still in daily use.

**The structural device of this page is the one thing that is hidden.** The paper list is never shown to the other people in the scene, and the laptop screen shows only grey bars. The reader should notice that the list lives only in the last panel.

## The character
Nora, mid-thirties, calm and careful, short dark hair, olive cardigan over a white shirt. Keep visually identical across all panels. Her colleague Pieter: tall, fair hair, grey sweatshirt. "Appearance is deliberately generic. Do not base on any real person. No place or organisation names."

## PANEL 1: THE BOARD
Heading with icon: **1. THE BOARD**
A small studio table. Nora (left) and Pieter (right) sit either side of one open laptop. Screen: grey bars only. Pieter is leaning in, frowning, one finger raised towards the screen.
Pieter's speech bubble: **"Where did the client's deadline go?"**

## PANEL 2: NOBODY KNEW
Panel 2 is the next moment of panel 1, same table, same seats. Nora looks at the screen, Pieter looks at her. A pause. No speech bubbles.

## PANEL 3: WEEKS LATER
Vertical gutter text, hand-lettered: **LATER...**
Nora alone at the same table in softer light, writing on a small notepad with a pencil. Her laptop is open beside her, screen grey bars only, and it is turned away from the notepad.
Thought bubble (Nora): **"Just in case."**

## PANEL 4: THE LIST
Close on Nora's hand sliding a folded sheet of paper into the inside of a notebook. In the background, out of focus, Pieter is working at his laptop and does not look up. Nobody speaks. The paper shows no legible words.

## Storytelling requirements
Same two characters throughout, no humour, nobody is a villain, no narration. Arc: a gap appears → nobody can explain it → one person quietly insures against it. Above all, make Panel 4 quietly powerful.

## Lettering block
- The only lettering is the four panel headings, "LATER...", and the two lines above, spelled exactly as given.
- No title above the panels. No captions.
- Only one speech or thought bubble per speaker in each panel, and nobody else speaks. The bubble tail points to the speaker.
- Screens show grey bars only: no squares, codes, icons or legible text.
- No logos, wall charts, badges or labels.
- Four panels in one row, left to right.

## What this page must not say
- No mention of "template" and no suggestion that a feature would fix the problem.
- No product name, no company name, no numbers.
- No result: do not show whether the paper list was ever used.
- No judgement of the team or of Nora ("I don't know. You're the one asking" stays out).
- No mechanism the source lacks (no backups, no sync icons, no client).

PROMPT END
