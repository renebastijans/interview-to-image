# Code of conduct

This is a small, free project. The aim is a friendly, practical place to share what works with interview-to-image prompts.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and be patient with newcomers and with non-native English.
- Give specific, useful feedback and say what you tested.
- Respect the privacy of research participants: never post real interview data, and do not try to identify anyone from examples.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination or threats.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or repeated off-topic posts.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space connected to this repository.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), marking it as a conduct report, or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment. The maintainer will review reports as soon as possible, keep them confidential, and may edit or remove content, warn, or block people from the repository. There is no guaranteed response time.
